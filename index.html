How FastBridge works

One transaction

Every chain

Most bridges move funds from one chain at a time. FastBridge aggregates your balances across multiple source chains and delivers them in a single transaction funded and ready to trade.

FastBridge runs on intent-based execution. You define what you want to arrive with the protocol handles routing, gas, and settlement behind the scenes. No faucets, no gas management, no waiting. Most transactions settle in under 30 seconds.
